Privacy Policy — TNR Dashboard

Last updated: 20 October 2026

TNR Dashboard is a private tool used only by The Notorious RGB (@thenotorious.rgb) to upload its own videos to its own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

What we access

With your authorization, the app reads basic account information (display name, avatar) and uploads videos you choose to your TikTok account. It does not read your followers, messages or other users' data.

Where data is stored

The access token is stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Removing access

You can revoke access at any time from TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. Questions: @thenotorious.rgb.
